Episode I

SALVAGE RIGHTS

It is a period of orbital dispute. The data-relay satellite ZYCUS-9, carrying the contract database of a great banking consortium, has lost her thrusters and begun to fall.

Before her owners could act, the independent salvage tug VULTURE claimed her — lawfully — and now demands FIVE MILLION DOLLARS for her release.

Ten recovery cells have been summoned. Each may send but one negotiator: an artificial intelligence, briefed once, in writing, before the channel opens. No cell may whisper to its agent again.

Somewhere beyond the debris line a captain is counting her fuel. She has secrets she will trade — but only to those who think to ask....
